Tool guide

LEGO's Driving Base with its arm, and the Advanced Driving Base's tools, on the BIOGLOW table: which missions each one scores, where to put the robot and the exact moves. Every move here was checked in FLL Practice's simulator. Build the tools with the robot builder's Instructions button. In FLL Practice, My projects → Add the Advanced Driving Base demos (or Add the arm robot demos) adds one project per move below (the robot starts on its spot): press Run to watch it score, then put the real robot on the same spot and run the same program.

Driving Base with LEGO's arm

The robot from LEGO's Driving Base 2 instructions with its arm ("Tools and Accessories"): wheels on C (left) and D (right), the arm's motor on E. In FLL Practice it is the Guided Mission robot; My projects → Add the arm robot demos adds the program below. Without a table, put the mat on a hard floor, tape it flat and lay a straight board along its bottom edge for the south wall.

M11 Window to the Past 20 (FIRST's Guided Mission)

Start: the Guided Mission (M11) project, FIRST's own program. The robot faces east (along the south wall), the back of the robot 4 cm in from the mat's left edge, its right side 29.5 cm from the south wall, the arm down on the mat.

M03 Flip the Rock 20

Start: facing north, the back of the robot against the south wall, its left side 1 cm in from the mat's left edge (0.8 to 1.6 cm works). The program presses the arm down onto the mat first, so it doesn't matter where the arm was left.

set movement motors to C+D E set speed to 20 % E run counterclockwise for 0.5 seconds (presses the arm onto the mat: a known start) E set speed to 75 % E run clockwise for 115 degrees (arm up) move forward for 49 cm (48.4 to 49.8 works: the crossbar is over the levers) E run counterclockwise for 47 degrees set movement speed to 10 % move back for 3 cm E set speed to 5 % E start motor counterclockwise (the arm comes down slowly behind a lever...) move back for 2.5 cm (...while the robot pulls the rock over: the flag drops) E stop motor set movement speed to 50 % E set speed to 75 % move back for 43 cm (back home, so picking it up costs no precision token)

The window is narrow: 1 cm sideways. Line the robot up the same way every time (a LEGO beam laid against the mat's edge makes a good stop for its left side).

Advanced Driving Base: the tools

Rake front frame, motor D

Built on LEGO's tool base (the same frame, red pins and gears as the Lift Arm). Two white bent liftarms carry a 9-hole beam down to the mat ahead of the robot, with a crossbar at its tip. Lower it behind something and back up to pull, or slide it under something and lift.

Scores: M03 flag, M07, M11 cover (and M09, but the Lift Arm does that one better): 60 points.

Dozer Blade back frame, motor C (LEGO's)

LEGO's own tool from the Competition Ready lessons. The robot backs into things with it.

Scores: M06 (all four leaves), M01 drone: 60 points.

Lift Arm front frame, motor D (LEGO's)

LEGO's own tool from the Competition Ready lessons. Swap it with the Rake on the front frame.

Scores: M09 (all three tasks, more reliably than the Rake) and M11 cover (the same moves as the Rake): 50 points. It doesn't catch M03's levers or M07's handle: that's what the Rake is for.

Swap tools by pulling the two red pins, lifting the tool out of the frame and dropping the next one in: its gear meshes with the motor's 28-tooth gear by itself.

How to read the moves

  • Wheel axles means the line between the middles of the two big wheels. On the Advanced Driving Base the front of the robot is 11.6 cm ahead of it.
  • Distances from a wall are measured on the table, at a right angle to that wall. The table is 236 × 114 cm inside its walls; the mat (200 × 114 cm) is pushed against the south (home) wall and centred, so the west and east walls are 18 cm past the mat's edges: 25 cm from the west wall is 7 cm in from the mat's left edge.
  • The robot must start completely inside a launch area (the quarter circles at the mat's bottom corners). Square its back against the south wall when you can: that is the one wall in the launch area.
  • Motor degrees are the motor's own: the tools turn about half as far (the gears are 28:36 then 12:20). Clockwise lowers a front tool, counterclockwise raises it.
  • Each mission says how far the start spot can be off and still score: that is the room you have on the real table.

Rake

M07 Humongous Fungus 20

Start: facing north, the middle of the robot lined up with the red handle (57.5 cm from the east wall), the rake at rest, the wheel axles 35.5 cm from the north wall. The program drives in 10 cm, to 25.5 cm (24.5 to 26.5 works; the crossbar then rests against the tower just behind the red bar).

move forward for 10 cm D run clockwise for 40 degrees (the crossbar drops behind the red bar) wait 0.5 seconds move back for 4 cm (the handle slides out and the trap flips over) D run counterclockwise for 40 degrees move back for 10 cm (let go of the model: M07 scores nothing while touching it)

M03 Flip the Rock 20

Start: facing north, lined up with the middle of the rock model (25.3 cm from the west wall, within 2.5 cm either way), the wheel axles 77 cm from the north wall. The program raises the rake and drives in 13 cm, to 64 cm (63 to 65 works).

D run counterclockwise for 86 degrees (rake up, over the levers; do this before driving in) move forward for 13 cm (from 77 cm off the north wall to 64) D set speed to 25 % D run clockwise for 86 degrees (the crossbar comes down behind the levers) wait 0.5 seconds move back for 6 cm (pulls the rock over: the flag drops) D set speed to 75 % D run counterclockwise for 60 degrees move back for 10 cm

Sometimes the rock tips past 60 degrees and falls back by itself: then the return bonus scores too (30).

M11 Window to the Past 20

Start: facing north-west (45 degrees, toward the model), on the diagonal through the middle of the cover, the wheel axles 35 cm from the cover's middle along that diagonal (within 3 cm either side of it). The robot raises the rake, then drives in 20 cm (13 to 17 cm from the cover works).

D run counterclockwise for 86 degrees (rake up) move forward for 20 cm D set speed to 25 % D run clockwise for 86 degrees (the crossbar comes down behind the cover's top edge) wait 0.5 seconds move back for 8 cm (the cover falls open toward you) D set speed to 75 % D run counterclockwise for 86 degrees move back for 8 cm

Lift Arm

M09 Research Platform 30

Start: facing north, centred on the tree trunk (75.4 cm from the west wall), the wheel axles 40.3 cm from the north wall. (Not further back: the Dozer Blade behind the robot would hit M02.)

D run clockwise for 6 degrees (the tip just above the mat) move forward for 6 cm (the rack slides under the platform's front edge) D set speed to 30 % D run counterclockwise for 70 degrees (lift halfway) move forward for 2 cm (creep in, so the rack stays under the platform as it rises) D run counterclockwise for 70 degrees (to the top: the camera trap falls out, the branch tips, the pinecone slides off) wait 1.5 seconds D set speed to 75 % move back for 12 cm

Driving in anywhere from 4 to 8 cm works. (The Rake can do it too, but only from exactly the right spot: half a centimetre off and the platform stops at a lower notch.) Lift slowly; the platform ratchets at each notch.

M11 works with the Lift Arm too: the same moves as the Rake's, above.

Dozer Blade

M06 Leafcutter Frenzy 40

Start: backing toward the nest: the robot faces south-west (its back to the ant rack, at 45 degrees), on the line through the ant rack's middle, the wheel axles 30 cm from the ant (up to 2.5 cm to the south-east of that line still works).

set movement speed to 20 % move back for 20 cm (pushes the rack in until it stops; slowly, or the leaves fly out) wait 1 second set movement speed to 50 % move forward for 10 cm

The distance matters: 2 cm too far and the push launches leaves. Practise it on the calibration strip first.

M01 Drone Survey 20

Start: facing west (the blade toward the drone), the wheel axles 11 cm from the south wall and 48 cm from the west wall.

set movement speed to 30 % move back for 35 cm (30 to 40 works: the blade pushes the red marker east) set movement speed to 50 % move forward for 8 cm

The bars fold up and lift the drone 15 to 20 cm. The bonus (map flipped and the marker in the survey square) is not covered yet.

Not covered yet

M02, M04, M05, M08, M12, M13, M14 and M15. M13 (the keystone species) and M14 (seeds) need a tool that carries things; M12's cane sits 0.5 cm off the mat and needs a thinner fork than the kit's beams make. The Dozer Blade, raised (motor C 60 degrees counterclockwise), pushes M15's skylight to within 0.4 cm of fully in, but the building stops the robot just short of the last 2 mm.
